About

I work at the intersection of trustworthy AI, medical imaging, and physical systems. My current work spans offline medical-image analysis, auditable model evaluation, and robot-control prototyping.

Alongside research software, I build the surrounding experimental infrastructure: data and label conversion, frozen benchmark units, failure analysis, provenance tracking, and research/operator interfaces. I am especially interested in what should happen before a model's output becomes a physical action.

Research Assistant

Project 1 · MRI-TRUS Image Processing and Robot Control for Ultrasound-Guided Prostate Biopsy

  • Developed Python control prototypes for an Inovance IR-U8-series collaborative robot over TCP and a Beckhoff ADS puncture stage, integrating motion commands, position/state polling, target and timeout register writes, tolerance-based stop logic, and error/status feedback; built a unified Electron/React operator interface.
  • Designed and executed an optical-tracking RCM repeatability study across 0, 3.5, 5, and 8 N loads; analyzed 197 quality-controlled line observations and obtained a report-defined pooled repeatability range (RPall) of 0.53—0.87 mm.
  • Built an offline prostate-MRI segmentation and MRI-TRUS registration research-software pipeline using MONAI pretrained models and ITKElastix, implementing geometry validation, 0.5-mm preprocessing, sliding-window inference, native-space restoration, signed-distance staged registration, Dice/HD95/TRE and Jacobian-based regional deformation checks, quality control, provenance tracking, and Python/CLI/local-HTTP interfaces.
  • On a frozen 40-case PROMISE12 evaluation set, the segmentation pipeline improved mean Dice from 0.77 to 0.85 and reduced HD95 from 18.65 to 5.31 mm versus the MONAI whole-volume baseline. Evaluated the MRI-TRUS registration workflow across nine mu-RegPro pairs from five MRI groups using contour Dice, HD95, centroid TRE, and Jacobian-based deformation checks.

Project 2 · Low-SNR Sensor Time-Series Parameter Estimation and Reliability Modelling

Project 3 · Surgical Scene Semantic Segmentation and Auditable Model Adaptation (MediSegMan)

  • Built and independently executed an auditable offline surgical-scene segmentation pipeline across five public datasets; adapted the public SegMAN implementation and evaluated five public comparison pipelines under dataset-specific protocols. Completed 17 frozen benchmark units covering dataset/label conversion, video-/case-level split checks, validation-based checkpoint selection, grouped metrics, and result provenance.
  • Integrated CoordAtt/ECA at the P5/P11 SegMAN decoder stages and evaluated a role-grouped residual-logit recalibration variant. Ran training-side comparisons using class-weighted cross-entropy, Dice, OHEM, and AdapterSIS teacher-logit distillation, and built all-class, foreground, per-class, and role-group metrics with video-/case-level paired bootstrap analyses.
  • Completed CaDIS Task I data conversion for 25 videos/4,670 total frames, a preregistered video-level split, three-method training, validation-based checkpoint freezing, and sealed testing. Independently recomputed confusion matrices and verified SHA-256 prediction hashes; across SegMAN, A6, and A6+TAB, sealed-test mIoU ranged from 86.65% to 86.93%.
